Solve this school maths or science problem. Work it out step by step in a few short lines. Then end with exactly one line that starts with "FINAL:" and gives every value the question asks for, with its unit, for example "FINAL: 5 m/s^2" or "FINAL: t1 = 3 s, t2 = 4 s". If there is nothing to calculate, end with "FINAL: none".
